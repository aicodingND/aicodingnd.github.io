Conservation · Driftless Wisconsin

No. II

How it works

three steps, written-first
  1. 01

    Send the brief

    Two or three sentences about your business in plain English. The intake form at the bottom of this page takes about ninety seconds. No call, no calendar, no sales script.

    Within 2 business days
  2. 02

    Get a fixed-price proposal

    A written one-pager: the page or site I would build, the price, the delivery window, the revision policy, what is in scope and what is out. Read it on your own time. Reply yes, reply with edits, or walk.

    Then a 40% deposit invoice
  3. 03

    Two to three weeks later, you have a site

    Custom design, written-for-you copy, deployed to your domain. Two written revision rounds. If anything is unclear, we stay in writing, so you can answer it when your day allows. Final invoice on launch.

    Hand-off documentation included
