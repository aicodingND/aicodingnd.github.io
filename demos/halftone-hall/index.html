    Before you come

    Bring a small bag, an ID if you want a drink, and earplugs. Forgot them? Free at both bars.

    Doors

    Doors at 7 pm, first band at 8 pm, headliner usually by 9:30 pm. Late shows are marked on the bill. We aim to have the house lights up by 11:30 pm on weeknights so you can catch the last bus.

    Ages

    All ages unless a show says 18+ or 21+. Under 16 needs a parent or guardian in the building. Bar wristbands at the door with a valid photo ID; no wristband, no drink, no exceptions.

    Bags

    Clear bags up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches, or a small clutch up to 4.5 by 6.5 inches. No backpacks, no camera bags, no professional cameras with detachable lenses. Phones are fine. Coat check is $3, November through March.

    Ears

    Free foam earplugs at both bars and the merch table. We keep the floor mix at a level our own sound crew can stand for four nights a week.

    Getting here

    Two pay lots on Market Street within a block, free street parking after 6 pm, and a covered bike rack for 20 by the side door. Rideshare drop-off is the loading zone on Shelby Street.

    Accessible seating

    1. Main floorStanding, 480. Flat concrete, no steps from the door to the rail.
    2. Accessible platformRaised 18 inches on house left, halfway back, so you see over the crowd. 12 wheelchair spaces, each with a fixed companion seat beside it.
    3. Step-free entranceMarket Street door, ramped at 1 in 14, 36 inch clear opening, staffed from doors to close.
    4. BarA lowered 34 inch section at the south end, and table service to the platform if you flag any staffer.
    5. Accessible restroomsTwo single-user, all-gender rooms with grab bars, on both levels.
    6. Lift to balconyFits a standard wheelchair plus one companion. Balcony has 4 wheelchair spaces on the front rail.
    7. Balcony above120, standing rail plus 40 stools. Covers the back third of the room.

    How to book it. Choose "Accessible platform" when you buy tickets. It costs the same as general admission and includes one companion ticket at no charge. You do not need to show proof of anything.

    Need something else? ASL interpretation, a reserved seat on the floor near an exit, or a sensory-friendly quiet spot during the set: email access@halftonehall.com or call the box office. ASL needs 14 days notice so we can book a pair of interpreters.

    Rent the pressroom

    Album releases, company holiday parties, wedding receptions, a film premiere, a 40th birthday with a real band. Our own crew runs sound and lights for every rental, so the room sounds like it does on a Saturday.
