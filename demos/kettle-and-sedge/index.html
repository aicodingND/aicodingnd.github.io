A narrow lane, on purpose.

We plant what was here before the lawn, and we build the stone, soil and water work that lets it live. That is the whole job. Doing one thing is how we can promise it will still look right in year five.

  4. 3 growing seasons, and it keeps going

    Establish

    Sleep, creep, leap

    Native plantings follow an old rule: the first year they sleep, the second they creep, the third they leap. Year one looks thin above ground while roots go down three feet. We stay on it the whole time.

    • Two visits in year one are included: June and September
    • Weeding and cutback visits in years two and three, priced per visit
    • Any plug that fails in its first season is replaced at no charge

The bar is drawn closer to real time than most firms draw it, and it is still generous to the first three stages. At true scale the consult would be a hairline. A large lakeshore buffer can run the Plan stage to ten weeks when a permit is involved.

    Questions we hear on every visit.

    Will it look messy?

    In year one, a little, and we say so up front. We draw every planting with a clean mown edge, a path or a low border so it reads as intended from the street. Neighbors notice the edge before they notice the plants.

    Is this allowed in my city?

    Both Minneapolis and Saint Paul allow native plantings with conditions about height near sidewalks and corners. Suburbs vary. We check the rules for your address during the Plan stage and draw to them.

    Can you help with grant programs?

    When a state or watershed cost-share round is open, we will help you fill in the plan and plant list portions. We do not control those programs or their timing, so we never price a project assuming you will win one.

    What if plants die?

    Any plug that fails in its first growing season is replaced at no charge on the September visit. After that, losses are usually about the site, and we will tell you why and what we would change.

    Why do you charge for the consultation?

    Because it is real work, and because it keeps our calendar for people who are serious. You get written notes and a plant list either way, and the fee comes off the design if you go ahead.
