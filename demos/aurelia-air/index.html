06 / Stewardship
[ 06 / STEWARDSHIP ]
Eleanor Vance
Founder & Chief Stewardship Officer
We do not sell speed. We return the hours a journey usually takes from you, and we treat each one as borrowed, not owed.

Aurelia began with a simple, stubborn idea. The point of a private aircraft is not the aircraft. It is the morning meeting you keep and the dinner at home you still make. The cabin is only the room where those hours are kept whole. So we built the operation backwards from that, starting with the Flight Team and the safety floor, and letting the fleet follow.

A membership is a piece of trust we are asked to steward. That is why the terms read plainly, the rate holds for the year, and the same person answers the second call as the first. Status display ages badly. The quiet competence of a plan that simply works does not.
