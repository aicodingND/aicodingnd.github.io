Wood-fired kitchen · Southside, Chattanooga

Lit before dawn. Cooked all the way down.

We light one oven at 5:40 every morning and cook the whole menu as it rises and falls: bread at first heat, vegetables on the roasting floor, braises when the door goes on, flatbreads at the service fire, and dessert on whatever warmth is left overnight.

One fire. Five heats. One menu.

A wood oven never holds a single temperature. It climbs for two hours after lighting, falls slowly through the afternoon, is built back up for dinner, and gives off heat all night after the coals come out. Every dish we serve was planned for a point on this line. The marker shows where the oven is about now.
