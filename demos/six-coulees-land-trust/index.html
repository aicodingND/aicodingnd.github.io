Where you can walk, where you can fish, and where you can only look.

Most of what we protect is private farmland under easement: the family keeps farming, we hold the development rights. Please admire those from the road. The preserves and stream corridors are yours.

    Membership

    An easement lasts forever.

    So does the job of keeping it. Members pay for the closings, and for the visit we make to every easement, every year, to check the deal is still being kept.

    Membership is any gift of $35 or more in a year, once or spread monthly. Every member gets the printed trail map, the work-day calendar, and a vote at the annual meeting in the Halvor town hall.
