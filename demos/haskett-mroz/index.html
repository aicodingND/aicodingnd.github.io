Monthly books

For businesses with a bank account and a pulse

We reconcile every account, sort every transaction, and send a two-page report by the 12th of the next month. Each quarter we spend twenty minutes on the phone walking you through it.

What moves the fee: how many accounts and how many transactions. A café with one bank account and one card sits near the bottom.

Payroll & sales tax

Your schedule, our filings

We run payroll weekly, every two weeks, or twice a month. We file the quarterly and year-end payroll returns, send W-2s and 1099s in January, and handle Wisconsin sales tax.

What moves the fee: headcount and how often you pay people.

$180 to $420per month

Business tax return

S-corporation, partnership or C-corporation

The federal and Wisconsin returns for the company, and a K-1 for every owner. If we keep your books, the return is mostly done by February.

What moves the fee: number of owners, other states, and whether we start from our books or someone else's.

$1,800 to $3,400per year

Household return

You, and the K-1 from your business

Your personal federal and Wisconsin returns, with the business income folded in correctly. Grown children and parents can be added to the same sheet at their own fixed fee.

What moves the fee: rental property, stock sales, other states.

$650 to $1,400per year

Year-round planning

So April holds no surprises

Two meetings a year: one in the quiet summer months, one before December. We project your tax, set your quarterly estimates, and tell you which moves are worth making before the year closes.

Advisory projects

The decisions that come once a decade

Choosing or changing your entity, setting what owners pay themselves, buying or selling a business, bringing in a partner, passing the company to the next generation. Scoped in writing, priced before we begin.

$2,500 to $7,500per project

How the first meeting goes

Forty-five minutes, kept on a timesheet.

No pitch deck, no tour of the software. We spend the time on your business and finish with a number on paper.

  1. 0:00 to 0:10

    You talk, we listen.

    What the business does, who is on payroll, what keeps you up in March.

  2. 0:10 to 0:30

    We read last year with you.

    Your returns, line by line where it matters. We point out anything that looks costly or risky, in plain words.

  3. 0:30 to 0:40

    We say what we would do.

    Which services you need, which you do not, and what we would change first.

  4. 0:45

    We write the number down.

    A fixed annual fee, sent in writing within two business days. Take it or leave it, no follow-up calls.

Bring, if you have them
  • Last year's business and personal returns
  • A recent profit and loss, even a rough one
  • Any letters from the IRS or the state you are unsure about

Before you call

What you can check, and what we don't do.

No badges, no star ratings. Kept the way we keep your books: both sides, in plain view.

Credits Look these up

  • Both partners hold active Wisconsin CPA licenses.Look them up yourself in the state's public license search. Our license numbers are printed on every engagement letter.
  • Every fee is in writing before work starts.The engagement letter lists each service, its fixed fee, and what would change it.
  • References, on request.Ask, and we will connect you with two clients in a business like yours, once they have said yes.
  • One business day to answer.Two in March and April. We cap the practice at 140 clients so that stays true.

Debits By choice

  • No awards.We have not entered any. A plaque would not tell you whether we answer the phone.
  • No review stars.We do not run a ratings widget, and we never trade anything for a review.
  • No audits.We do not perform audits or other attest work. If a lender asks for audited statements, we will refer you to a firm that does them well.
  • No crypto or cross-border returns.We would be learning on your time. We will point you to someone who already knows.
